# Authority Boundary V1

## Commercial authority

`MANASAKNA_BUSINESS` may administer company-owned commercial Umrah
operations, including tenants, branches, staff, plans, customers and future
commercial operational modules.

## Prohibited sovereign Hajj authority

The commercial project must never create, change, reinterpret, or own:

- Hajj eligibility;
- Hajj lottery or allocation;
- Hajj quota;
- official Hajj pilgrim status;
- government Hajj records;
- government Hajj audit authority.

A future company role in Hajj may only operate through a separately governed,
bounded delegated contract. Delegation never converts the commercial system
into the sovereign owner.

## Integration rule

Future exchange with `MANASAKNA_APP` is contract-only, versioned,
least-privilege, provenance-preserving and auditable.

Direct cross-plane table access and implicit joins are prohibited.
